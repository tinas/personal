# About Me

Hi, I’m Ahmet. I’m a software developer who enjoys building things that feel useful, clean, and meaningful. Most of what I know comes from working on real projects, solving real problems, and learning from talented developers who helped shape the way I approach my work.

I believe that software development has its own version of the master and apprentice mindset. I learn best by doing, exploring, and improving alongside others. I enjoy refactoring complex areas, simplifying messy parts of a codebase, improving user experience, and creating structures that are easier to understand and maintain.

I also build open-source tools and npm packages based on the challenges I face in my daily workflow. These projects keep me motivated, push me to learn new techniques, and allow me to share what I discover with the community.

When I am not writing code, I enjoy discovering new music, walking around the city, experimenting in the kitchen, and exploring interface ideas through design tools.

This website is where I share what I learn, what I build, and what interests me along the way.
