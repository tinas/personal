# About Me

Hi, I'm Ahmet. I'm a software developer who cares about making things simpler, whether that means refactoring a tangled codebase, improving a confusing user flow, or building a structure that's easier to reason about.

Most of what I know comes from working on real projects and learning alongside talented people. I believe in the master and apprentice approach to software. You grow fastest when you build, break, and improve things with others.

I also build open-source tools based on problems I run into during my daily work. Projects like [qpick](https://github.com/tinas/qpick), [fakend](https://github.com/tinas/fakend), and [qubo](https://github.com/tinas/qubo) started that way. Small frustrations that turned into something worth sharing.

When I'm not writing code, I'm usually discovering new music, walking around the city, experimenting in the kitchen, or sketching interface ideas in design tools.
